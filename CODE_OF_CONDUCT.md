# Code of Conduct

weighme is built in the open, by and for people who care about what they ship. We want taking part in it to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere, and a question is a contribution too.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, in issues and in code review.
- Keep discussions on topic and focused on making the project better.
- Handle security information responsibly: report vulnerabilities privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), never share exploits for unpatched issues in public threads.
- Talk about packages and their maintainers the way you would want yours talked about. A large bundle is a measurement, not a judgement of the people behind it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@janszotkowski](https://github.com/janszotkowski)). Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a participant, depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior.
